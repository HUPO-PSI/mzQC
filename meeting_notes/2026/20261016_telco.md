## PRIDE QC Metrics (Justin)

- Metrics computed directly from raw data: MS1/MS2 counts, chromatography duration, MS1/MS2 tolerance estimates (ParamMedic approach), frequent PTM mass shifts, TMT channel/spectra counts.
- Works for DDA, DIA and TMT. PRM/SRM untested.
- About 10-15 metrics, mostly implemented in OpenMS, all using existing CV terms.
- One mzQC per raw file, optionally merged per Pride accession then passed to pmultiQC for the report.
- Goals: SDRF enrichment (tolerances, PTMs) and search filters on the Pride archive.
- Feedback:
  - David: tolerances matter less now that search engines optimize them.
  - Wout: tolerances and PTMs overlap with the metadata effort (RunAssessor); coordinate to avoid duplication. 
  - Condense to metrics useful for ~95% of datasets, with at least one per MS stage (separation, ionization, acquisition).
